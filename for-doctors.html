The digital prescription loop.

We removed the paper, the lost forms, and the patient non-compliance. Here is how LabRunr connects you directly to the diagnostic process.

1

Digital Request

Select tests from our catalogue of 150+ options. Enter the patient's phone number. LabRunr sends them a secure SMS link to book their home collection.

2

We Handle the Rest

The patient chooses their preferred accredited lab and books a Runr. We collect the sample at their home and deliver it to the lab. You track the status live.

3

Instant Results

The moment the processing lab verifies the results, they are pushed simultaneously to the patient's app and your clinical dashboard. No more chasing down PDFs.

2. Submit Folio

3. Ops Verification

Our clinical operations team verifies your credentials within 24 hours to unlock your dashboard.
